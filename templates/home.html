Zarkali Lab

We are a computational cognitive neuroscience lab based at the Dementia Research Centre at University College London, led by Dr Angeliki Zarkali.

Our aim is to understand the brain circuits responsible for cognitive decline in Lewy body disease and develop novel approaches to treatments.

To do this, we use a combination of multimodal neuroimaging, cognitive and behavioural tests, fluid biomarkers and non-invasive stimulation techniques such as focused ultrasound.

For examples of the research we do, see research and publications.
