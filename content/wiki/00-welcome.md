# Welcome to the lab

This wiki is the shared operating manual for the lab. It should answer routine questions quickly and make our working practices visible to everyone. 
Answers to most initial questions will be found here or the lab sharepoint. 

## What belongs here

- vision, values and standards
- onboarding and access instructions
- lab meetings and recurring routines
- standard practices on analysis, supervision and publication
- practical tips that otherwise disappear into chat or email

## How to use the wiki

Use search on the wiki landing page, or browse by category. Every page is plain Markdown in `content/wiki/`, so edits can be made directly on GitHub and reviewed through pull requests.

## Getting started
On your first day Angelika or a member of the team will meet you and show you around the lab and different buildings. 
Make sure to do these things within the first week of getting started:
### General checklist:
1. Get your access card
2. Complete building induction (Russel Square house and FIL)
3. IT induction (check sharepoint guide for list)
4. Fire safety inductions
5. Mandatory UCL induction
6. Scanning safety session at 12 Queen Square (if you’ll be doing any scanning with us)

### Lab checklist:
1. Read the lab wiki
2. Get added to the lab Teams channel (ask Angelika)
3. Get added to the lab Calendar (ask Angelika)
4. Get added to shared research drives
5. Apply for an account on ERN https://ern.ucl.ac.uk/ - once you have your account ask Angelika to add you to the lab notebook
6. Get a UCL HPC account
7. Write a short biography to be added to the lab’s website
8. Get a GitHub account and link it to the lab’s GitHub at https://github.com/Zarkali-lab
