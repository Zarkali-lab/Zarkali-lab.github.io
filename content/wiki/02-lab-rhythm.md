# Lab rhythm & meetings

## Monday stand-up — 30 min

Fast coordination meeting. Each person covers: what moved last week, priority for this week, blockers, and any decision needed from the group.

Keep project deep-dives out of stand-up. Capture actions and move technical discussion to a smaller follow-up.

## Friday wrap-up — 30 min

Close the loop on the week. Share one useful result, one unresolved problem, and any work that should be visible before the following Monday.

This is also a good slot for very short demos, figures, pipeline changes or papers worth discussing.

## One-to-ones

Use one-to-ones for supervision, development, project decisions and concerns that do not belong in a group meeting. Send figures or drafts beforehand when useful.
